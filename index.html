Alerts, not tickets.

We are an independent alert service. Here is exactly what that means — and what it does not mean.

We do not sell tickets.

We have no tickets, no inventory and no allocation of any kind.

We do not resell tickets.

We are not a reseller and we are not a secondary marketplace.

We do not purchase tickets for you.

We never buy on your behalf or hold anything in your name.

We do not guarantee availability.

Availability can change quickly, and an alert is never a promise.

You always complete your purchase yourself through Arsenal’s official ticketing process.

Tickets remain subject to Arsenal’s availability, membership eligibility, ticketing rules, and official purchase process.
